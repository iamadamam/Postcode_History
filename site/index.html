NI Postcode History

Monuments, historic buildings and old maps near any Northern Ireland postcode.

What's near you?

Enter a postcode to see everything recorded by the Department for Communities within 1 km: archaeological monuments, historic buildings, industrial heritage and historic parks and gardens. Switch on a historical map to see the area as it was surveyed between 1829 and 1963.

You can also enter just the first part of a postcode, such as BT48, or try BT7 1NN (Queen's University, Belfast).
